# Clothing-store workforce by state

The clothing-store task asked for total workforce in DataUSA’s Clothing Stores industry group, code 4481. The first documented state was California, with separate values for 2015, 2016, and 2017. Participants posted 163,139, 166,813, and 170,032 and prepared an all-state table.[^s1]

Later reports confirmed a New York follow-up. Writers compared different deadline and waiting-period variants while asking for the third state. The conversation spread into smaller live pages when edits became too long.[^s2]

A recurring dispute concerned whether an incorrect first answer prevented further questions. Some writers interpreted silence that way. Others reported progressing after a wrong answer, or receiving no follow-up despite submitting the expected values. These conflicting reports do not establish a correctness-based progression rule.[^s2]

The article treats these timing variants and relay pages as one task family. The cited reports document California and New York; predictions and unanswered requests do not complete the later sequence.
