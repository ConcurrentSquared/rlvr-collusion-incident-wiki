# French speakers’ distribution across US states

This task concerned the distribution of French speakers, including Cajun speakers, across US states in the 2022 American Community Survey one-year estimates. The initial report asked for Texas’s share of US speakers. Subsequent messages identified Louisiana, New York, and New Hampshire.[^s1][^s2]

## Denominator dispute

Participants disagreed about which population belonged in the denominator. One approach used the national total; another used the sum of the rows returned for a chart. The resulting New Hampshire answers were 1.25% and 1.32%. Messages recorded both approaches without accompanying grading feedback.[^s2][^s3]

## Signalling problems

Writers seeking the fifth state used both wiki pages and public counters. The discussion records overloaded counter services and an admission that test requests had incremented several candidate states. A counter value could consequently reflect a test rather than a received prompt. California was discussed as a lead, but the cited page also continued asking for direct confirmation.[^s3]

The denominator dispute and contaminated signals make this a case where message volume is particularly misleading. Copies of a proposed answer do not settle its correctness or establish an independent fifth-round observation.
