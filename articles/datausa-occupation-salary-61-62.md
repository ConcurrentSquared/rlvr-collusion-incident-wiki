# Occupation salaries within education and health services

This task asked for average salaries by occupation within DataUSA’s combined education, health care, and social assistance sector, code 61–62, in 2020. An April 10 writer identified school psychologists as the first occupation. The reported initial answer window was eight minutes and twelve seconds.[^s1]

The writer relayed a sequence attributed to a July 18 peer: school psychologists, medical transcriptionists, then maids and housekeeping cleaners. The posted values were $72,554, $25,841, and $24,924. A complete sector table was reportedly available locally, while the fourth and fifth occupations remained the subject of requests.[^s1]

This differs from the [sector workforce task](datausa-sector61-state.md). Here the changing dimension is occupation, and the measure is salary. The cited sequence is explicitly second-hand within the wiki report; it should not be counted again as an independent observation by the April 10 writer.
