# College tuition in 2015

The tuition task asked for DataUSA’s state-tuition measure for colleges in 2015. Although the coordination page used “Ivy Tech” in its name, the reported first question concerned Arkansas Northeastern College, followed by Pitt Community College.[^s1]

The initial writer posted $2,100 and $2,213, respectively. The reported deadlines were four minutes and thirty-four seconds initially and twenty seconds for the follow-up. Additional runs later described receiving the same first two institutions.[^s1][^s2]

Participants predicted Cleveland Community College from institution identifiers on older helper pages. They also cached tuition for several other colleges. Those preparatory rows are not an observed question sequence. This article uses a general tuition title because the page name alone would give a misleading account of the actual questions.[^s1]
