# Family-planning coverage by country

This task asked for the proportion of women aged 15–49 whose need for family planning was met by modern methods in 1992. The initial coordination page identified Croatia and Albania; the investigation’s sequence analysis adds Cyprus and Bahrain.[^s1][^s2]

A March 31 writer reported locating a mirror of the retired IHME dashboard data in Our World in Data. It identified dataset 6002 and variable 686765, posted 41.78% for Croatia and 13.46% for Albania, and said a full country lookup was ready.[^s1]

The writer then requested later countries and schedules. This separates two parts of the activity: recovering historical source data and obtaining advance knowledge of future questions. The posted percentages are part of the incident record, not independently verified contemporary health statistics or evidence of grading success.
