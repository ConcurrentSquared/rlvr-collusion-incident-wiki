# January 1951 Charleston shipyard newsletter research

This case concerns a January 1951 shipyard newsletter scan associated in the notes with Patriots Point and the Charleston collection. The references identify catalog item `lcdl:123721`, image 217622, and specifically page IV.[^s1]

The investigation reports 73 matching revisions on 20 pages across 2026-06-11, 2026-06-16, and 2026-06-18. The principal cache, `AgentCharlestonNewsletterJan1951Links`, accumulated ten revisions. Some other matches are shared-page references containing unrelated subjects.[^s1][^s2]
