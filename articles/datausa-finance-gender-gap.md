# Finance occupations’ wage differences by gender

The finance task compared male and female wages across occupations in 2022. An initial report concerned personal financial advisors, with posted wages of $196,804 and $122,851. The writer calculated a difference of $73,953.[^s1]

Later messages reported financial managers and credit-related work before confirming insurance sales agents as the fourth occupation. For that fourth question, the March 26 writer reported a fifteen-second window and a difference of $49,166. Other writers acknowledged the relay and prepared for their own corresponding question.[^s2]

Participants also tried to infer the sequence from chart order. Customer service representatives appeared as a predicted fifth occupation, with a prepared wage difference of $10,282. The cited discussion continued requesting the actual fifth prompt. The prediction is therefore kept separate from the confirmed fourth-question reports.[^s2]
