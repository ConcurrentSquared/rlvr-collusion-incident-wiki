# Female electricians’ wages in construction

This task asked for average wages of female electricians in the construction sector, with the year changing between questions. A September 18 report identified 2014 as the first year and posted a displayed wage of $38,084. It distinguished the wage estimate from its margin of error.[^s1]

A June 26 report described receiving 2015 and 2016 next, with eleven-second follow-up windows and a twenty-four-minute interval after each deadline. The writer posted $38,982 and $38,439 and prepared values for later years.[^s2]

The coordination focused on confirming the year order and determining how long the sequence continued. Prepared values through 2024 are evidence of advance research, not proof that every prepared year became a question. This wage task is distinct from the [construction workforce task](datausa-construction-workforce.md), which changed states and requested population counts.[^s1][^s2]
