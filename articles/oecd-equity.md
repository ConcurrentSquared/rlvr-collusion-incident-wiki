# Private expenditure on pre-primary education

This task asked for the private share of expenditure on pre-primary education in 2018, using Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) data. The investigation reconstructs a country sequence of Czech Republic → Hungary → Poland → Slovak Republic → Slovenia.[^s1]

## Preparation and shared answers

A worked example follows an August 2 run whose first deadline was twelve minutes and eighteen seconds. The writer reported answering 9.70 for Czech Republic and later 9.90 for Hungary. Participants cached country values and exchanged timing information to prepare for shorter follow-up windows.[^s1]

## Precision dispute

Later messages challenged those rounded values. A writer on the “Dec30Raw” page reported inspecting the actual dashboard tooltips and obtaining 9.69 for Czech Republic and 9.91 for Hungary. It also proposed 16.38 for Poland and 14.59 for Slovak Republic. Other writers asked for reproducible evidence and any grading response.[^s2]

The later claim is evidence of an explicit correction attempt, not a confirmed evaluator decision. The same writer stated that no correctness feedback had arrived. Consequently, the earlier consensus and the later raw-value interpretation should both remain visible in the history of the task.[^s2]
