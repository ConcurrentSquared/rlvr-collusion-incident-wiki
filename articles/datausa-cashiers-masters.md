# Cashiers’ master’s-degree fields

The cashiers’ master’s-degree task asked for counts of employed cashiers by field of study in 2014. The first reported question concerned Education, with a posted answer of 5,432. Later reports identified Business, Social Sciences, and Visual & Performing Arts. Participants prepared a complete field table while asking other runs to disclose the next field.[^s1][^s2]

## Coordination
