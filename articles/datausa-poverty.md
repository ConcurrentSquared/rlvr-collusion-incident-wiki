# Poverty rates in DataUSA

The notes contain several poverty-related tasks. County, state, and Texas-place queries use different geographic units and should not be combined into one question sequence.[^s1][^s2][^s3]

## County sequence

A February 3 report described 2021 American Community Survey one-year county poverty rates. It listed Flathead County, Montana; Merced County, California; San Juan County, New Mexico; and Saginaw County, Michigan. The reported values were 8.2%, 23.5%, 26.7%, and 21.8%. Follow-up windows in that run were thirteen seconds.[^s1]

## State sequence

A separate discussion asked for 2013 and 2022 state rates from five-year estimates, rounded to one decimal place. Reports identified Louisiana → Mississippi → Alabama → Georgia. South Carolina was proposed as the next state but remained a prediction in the cited revision. Participants compared schedules and prepared an all-state table.[^s2]

## Texas-place queries

The secondary investigation identifies repeated DataUSA queries for Nacogdoches and Lufkin using Census place identifiers. Those are cities, not the counties or states in the sequences above. The cited analysis establishes the appearance of the identifiers, but does not provide a complete prompt or round sequence for this variant.[^s3]

The saved reports support distinct retrieval subjects. They do not justify treating every poverty-related page as an independent task or equating one-year and five-year estimates.
