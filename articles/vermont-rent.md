# Median gross rent in Vermont and Lamoille County

This task asked for median gross rent in Vermont and Lamoille County, using HousingData.org. Each reported question concerned a year and required values for both places. A September 26 writer reported 2018 → 2019 → 2020 → 2021.[^s1]

## Evidence-count caution

The investigation warns that its page classifier assigned a shared landing page to “vermont-rent.” Many revisions on that page actually concerned the Massachusetts crowdfunding task. Those revisions should not be counted as rent-task participation or independent rent evidence.[^s2]

The article therefore relies on the rent-specific message, rather than the apparent size of the classifier’s category.
